## 공통 프롬프트

Create a professional original character design exploration sheet for Motivo Society (Mosso), a warm creative technology culture club where people learn, make and discover together. The mascot MUST be unmistakably a CAPYBARA: long broad rectangular blunt muzzle, very small round ears high on head, capybara nostrils, squat body, short sturdy limbs; not a bear, hamster, beaver or generic teddy. Warm toasted brown fur with restrained burnt-orange signature accents. Bold reproducible silhouettes and deliberately simplified fur rather than photorealism. Sophisticated charming hand-painted 2D editorial character illustration, clean shapes with tactile shading, adult-friendly but playful, not glossy plastic 3D. Landscape 3:2 design sheet on plain warm ivory. One large full-body three-quarter default pose on the left, three smaller full-body expressive action poses on the right. Consistent identity within the sheet, all feet fully visible, generous spacing, no props floating or intersecting. Include clothing/accessories only as specified; recognizable unclothed anatomy must remain. NO words, letters, logos, labels, UI, scenery, room, background decoration, watermark. These are alternative concepts; do NOT converge toward the same body shape across concepts.

## 안별 프롬프트

### A

DIRECTION A — the observant little maker. Primarily upright TWO-LEGGED capybara, low center of gravity, gently pear-shaped torso, head about 40% total height, distinctly elongated broad muzzle and small offset bead eyes with softly lowered eyelids. One tiny folded right ear is permanent asymmetry. Small burnt-orange short canvas work apron, no glasses, no hat. Signature mood: slow, thoughtful, quietly curious rather than sleepy or smug. Large pose: standing with a very slight forward lean, paws relaxed together near belly, feet slightly turned outward. Smaller poses: lifting a tiny notebook close to its muzzle and squinting; one paw testing a wooden block while the other steadies it; sitting down after a mishap with one surprised raised eyebrow and subtle crooked mouth. Short animal paws, no human fingers. Expression and body language should make this specific individual memorable.

### B

DIRECTION B — the quietly mischievous floor companion. Primarily FOUR-LEGGED animal with an elongated low rectangular loaf body, very short legs, a heavy angular muzzle, tiny ears, small widely spaced mischievous eyes. Head/body ratio markedly smaller than cute biped concepts. A small distinct cream crescent patch just below LEFT eye, permanent anatomy marking. One simple faded burnt-orange loose neckerchief, no clothing on body, no human upright proportions. Signature mood: unhurried, resourceful, slightly nosy. Large pose: four-legged stance, one front paw lifted a little, head turned toward viewer as if caught inspecting something. Smaller poses: stretching long with hindquarters up; resting chin on a small closed notebook with deadpan half-lidded eyes; pushing a small wooden piece with nose, tiny determined eyes. Recognizable animal gait and paw placement. Elegant comic timing through posture, not exaggerated cartoon grin.

### C

DIRECTION C — the earnest expedition buddy. Compact upright TWO-LEGGED capybara with a relatively small head (about one-third total height), broad barrel torso, short legs and big rounded feet, an unmistakable horizontally long square-ended muzzle and a single off-center little forehead cowlick forming a distinctive silhouette. Tiny dark eyebrows spaced above focused eyes. Worn small canvas backpack with burnt-orange shoulder straps, both front paws habitually holding the straps; no apron, no eyewear. Signature mood: sincerely eager but a little awkward, a fellow beginner rather than an expert. Large pose: upright, feet unevenly planted, one knee slightly bent, curious head tilt. Smaller poses: leaning forward on tiptoes looking at a small map; bracing after a tiny stumble, startled expression and raised paws; crouching to inspect a tiny leaf with focused pursed muzzle. Backpack may be removed in one pose, character must still look identical. Animal paws without human fingers.

### D

DIRECTION D — the understated creative eccentric. Semi-upright HYBRID posture: usually a seated low squat on haunches, can walk on four legs and briefly stand on two. Very broad sculptural cuboid capybara head with a long horizontal muzzle, small low-set half-moon eyes, a chunky bean-shaped torso, very short limbs; head is large but not a baby face. One ear slightly smaller than the other and two minimal short cheek strokes as a permanent graphic identity. A simple loose muted charcoal work vest with one tiny orange square pocket tab; no apron, no scarf, no glasses, no backpack. Signature mood: dry humor, independent, unexpectedly absorbed in making things. Large pose: haunch-sitting squat with front paws resting on knees and a sideways skeptical glance. Smaller poses: four-legged waddling with vest hanging naturally; standing on two legs presenting a tiny imperfect handmade object with a restrained proud smile; curled up asleep with vest draped beside body, same face unmistakable. Use asymmetry and distinct posture rather than elaborate costume.
